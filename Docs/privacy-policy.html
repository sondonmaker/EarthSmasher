Privacy Policy

Earth Smasher · Sun Soft INC

This Privacy Policy (the "Policy") describes what information may be processed when you use Earth Smasher, and how that information is handled.

Sun Soft INC ("we", "us", or "the Company") treats user privacy seriously. We explain (1) why information is processed, (2) how it is stored, and (3) how it is protected.

Local-only app. Earth Smasher is a single-player sandbox game. Your gameplay data is stored on your device and is not uploaded to servers or databases operated by Sun Soft INC. We do not operate user accounts, cloud saves, or social features in this app.

By using Earth Smasher, you agree to this Policy. This Policy is effective as of January 1, 2025.

1. Information processed and how it is collected

(1) Information stored on your device

The app may save the following data locally to support gameplay:

  • Game progress: destroyed terrain, scorch marks, pierce holes, impact count, in-game population, simulation date/speed, and science points
  • Camera state: orbit camera position and rotation so your view can be restored
  • App settings: music volume, sound effects volume, and graphics quality (stored with Unity PlayerPrefs)
  • Device information (limited): operating system version and device type, used only to optimize rendering and performance on your device

Earth Smasher does not require a nickname, email address, phone number, contacts, photo library, camera roll, or location data to play.

(2) How information is collected

  • Automatically, while you play (for example, when the game saves your world state)
  • When you change in-app settings such as volume or graphics quality
  • When you use built-in save/load features provided by the game

We do not collect information through registration forms, advertising identifiers tied to your identity, or third-party analytics SDKs in the current version of Earth Smasher.

2. Use of processed information

Information stored on your device is used only to:

  • Provide core gameplay (destruction simulation, weapons, fleet attacks, save/load)
  • Remember your preferences (audio and graphics settings)
  • Maintain performance and stability on your device
  • Comply with applicable laws when legally required

We do not use your data for profiling, targeted advertising, or sale to data brokers.

3. Disclosure of information

Important: Your gameplay data stays on your device. Sun Soft INC does not receive or store it on company servers.

We do not disclose personal information to third parties except when:

  • You choose to share content using your device's system share sheet (outside our control)
  • Disclosure is required by law, regulation, or valid legal process

Earth Smasher does not include chat, friend lists, or automatic sharing of gameplay with other users. All content remains private on your device unless you explicitly share it through your operating system.

4. Cookies and tracking technologies

Earth Smasher is a native mobile game, not a website. It does not use browser cookies, web beacons, or third-party ad tracking that identifies you across apps or sites. We aim to provide a tracker-free experience.

5. Your rights and data retention

Because data is stored locally, you control it directly:

  • Access and correction: view and change settings inside the app at any time
  • Deletion: delete saved game data from within the app, or remove all app data in your device settings
  • Uninstall: removing the app deletes locally stored game files and settings from your device (subject to your OS behavior)

Retention: We do not keep a copy of your data on our servers. Data remains on your device until you delete it, clear app storage, or uninstall the application.

6. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information stored on your device:

  • We rely on the encryption and security features provided by your mobile operating system (Android / iOS)
  • The app requests only the permissions needed for gameplay; it does not access your photos, contacts, or microphone for core features
  • Save files are written to the app's private storage area on your device

7. Children's privacy

Earth Smasher is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because all data stays on the device, parents or guardians can remove a child's data by deleting save data or uninstalling the app.

8. Third-party services

The app may be distributed through platform stores (such as Google Play). Those platforms may process purchase, download, or device information under their own privacy policies. That processing is governed by the platform operator, not by Sun Soft INC, unless we explicitly integrate a third-party service in a future update. If we add such services, we will update this Policy.

9. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. When we do, the revised version will be posted at this URL and, where appropriate, noted in the app or store listing. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised Policy.
